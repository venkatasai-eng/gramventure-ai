# GramVenture AI — 3-Minute Hackathon Pitch Notes

**Problem Statement ID: 26091**  
**Title:** AI-Driven Hyper-Local Business Advisory and Financial Structuring Assistant for Rural Micro-Entrepreneurs  
**Tagline:** *"Your Village. Your Opportunity. Your Business Plan."*  
**Duration:** Exactly 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)

---

## ⏱️ Pitch Timeline & Script Breakdown

### 00:00 – 00:30 | Slide 1 & 2: The Critical Rural Dilemma
> **Speaker:**
> *"Respected judges, meet Suresh from Rampur village in Warangal. He has ₹1,00,000 saved from his harvest and wants to start a business.
> Under standard rural credit guidelines, that ₹1,00,000 qualifies him for a 90% loan on a ₹10,00,000 dairy unit.
> But here is the tragedy: over 68% of rural ventures face distress in their first 18 months. Why?
> First, they lack hyper-local ground evidence and copy neighbors blindly.
> Second, they conflate what a government scheme will sanction—say ₹9 Lakhs—with what their village cash-flow can actually repay.
> And third, generic chatbots hallucinate answers, claiming 'Yes, take ₹9 Lakhs, your loan is approved!'
> We built **GramVenture AI** to solve this."*

---

### 00:30 – 01:15 | Slide 3 & 4: The Product & 1-Click Live Demonstration
> **Speaker:**
> *"Our core product principle is simple: **Zero AI involvement in financial math.**
> All sizing, scheme limits, and amortization are executed in 100% deterministic TypeScript. Generative AI is strictly used for contextual plain-language advice in English and Telugu.
> 
> Let us look at our live demo for Rampur:
> 1. **Village Intelligence:** Suresh logs local ground evidence—2 informal milk vendors and 5 kirana shops within 3 km. Every number displays an explicit provenance badge: *Official Data, Verified Observation,* or *Model Estimate.* Our Evidence Coverage indicator shows 68% and explicitly lists what is missing.
> 2. **Business Comparison:** We compare 3 trades side-by-side—Dairy, Kirana, and Mobile Repair—across low, base, and high scenarios with transparent Capex and break-even timelines.
> 3. **Scheme Router:** Suresh’s project routes into the **Term Loan Scheme** (8% interest, 84-month tenure, 6-month moratorium). His theoretical loan is ₹9,00,000 with an illustrative ₹15,165 monthly EMI equivalent."*

---

### 01:15 – 02:00 | Slide 5 & 6: The Innovation — Cash Flow Stress Testing
> **Speaker:**
> *"Now comes our standout fintech innovation: **The Business Survival Simulator**.
> In the baseline case, Suresh has a healthy DSCR of 1.45.
> But what happens when summer hits, milk yield drops, or a buyer delays payment by 30 days?
> We move the sales slider down by 20%. Look at the screen:
> Because his fixed shed maintenance, animal feed, and household expenses do not drop, his DSCR plummets to **0.88**—a severe cash-flow deficit!
> 
> Instead of letting him borrow ₹9,00,000 blindly, GramVenture AI flashes an institution-grade warning:
> *'Your scheme-based maximum exceeds your cash-flow capacity.'*
> The engine algorithmically recommends resizing his project downward to **₹6,50,000**, buying 2 animals instead of 4, keeping his stressed DSCR above 1.15. That is responsible credit advisory."*

## 💡 Quick Q&A Defense for Judges

| Likely Judge Question | Recommended Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"Why not just let Gemini calculate the loan?"** | *"LLMs are probabilistic and suffer from arithmetic hallucinations. In rural lending, an error of 1% in reducing-balance amortization can lead to loan default. We keep math 100% deterministic and use LLMs only for vernacular explanation."* |
| **"Where do your local competitor numbers come from?"** | *"We never fabricate village statistics. If official block census data is unavailable, we state 'Missing Data' and provide an easy observation logger for the entrepreneur to record what they physically see in their village."* |
| **"How does the scheme routing handle boundaries?"** | *"Our scheme router cleanly evaluates the ₹1,40,000 ceiling. Projects ≤ ₹1,40,000 route to Micro Finance (6.5%, 3-year, 3-month moratorium, capped at ₹1,25,000). Projects above ₹1,40,000 route to Term Loan (8%, 7-year, 6-month moratorium, capped at ₹45,00,000). Over ₹50,00,000 is rejected with a clear explanation."* |
| **"Can this scale across India?"** | *"Yes! All scheme configurations and business templates are decoupled in external JSON/TypeScript registries. Adding new regional schemes or state corporative subsidies takes minutes without altering frontend components."* |
