# GramVenture AI — 3-Minute Live Hackathon Demo Checklist

Follow this quick guide to experience the complete end-to-end journey of GramVenture AI (Problem Statement ID: 26091).

---

## ⚡ 1-Click Instant Demo Option
1. Open the web app at `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Click the orange **"Explore Demo"** button on the top-right header or hero banner.
3. The app instantly preloads the stable, documented scenario for **Suresh in Rampur village, Warangal (₹1,00,000 margin capital)** and opens the workflow.

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Evaluator Walkthrough

### Step 1: Entrepreneur Onboarding (`/home`)
- [ ] Observe the **Headline**: *"Build a Smarter Business Before You Borrow."*
- [ ] Check the location selector: **Telangana ➔ Warangal ➔ Dharmasagar ➔ Rampur**.
- [ ] Verify **Margin Capital**: `₹1,00,000` (Indian number formatting ₹1,00,000 displayed).
- [ ] Check household finances: `₹12,000/mo` living expenses, `₹30,000` reserve.
- [ ] Verify 3 selected businesses: **Dairy Unit, Grocery / Kirana, Mobile Repair**.
- [ ] Click **"Proceed to Market Intelligence"**.

### Step 2: Hyper-Local Village Intelligence (`/village`)
- [ ] Verify the **Evidence Coverage Indicator**: Displays **68%** with transparent formula breakdown.
- [ ] Check **Evidence Badges**: *Official Data, Verified Local Observation, Model Estimate, Missing Data*.
- [ ] Review competitor observations: 2 informal dairy vendors, 5 bazaar kirana shops.
- [ ] Test the **Record New Observation** form: Enter a new shop count and click Save.
- [ ] Inspect the **Grounded SWOT Analysis** specifically generated for Dairy.
- [ ] Click **"Compare Business Models"**.

### Step 3: Deterministic Business Comparison (`/compare`)
- [ ] Notice the side-by-side comparison of **Dairy, Grocery, and Mobile Repair**.
- [ ] Toggle scenarios: Switch between **Low, Base, and High** cases to watch CapEx and margins update.
- [ ] Click **"Inspect Scoring Formula"** to verify the 5-pillar explainable ranking:
  - Financial Affordability: 30%
  - Local Opportunity: 25%
  - Competitive Positioning: 20%
  - Operational Risk: 15%
  - Capital Fit: 10%
- [ ] Click **"View Itemized CapEx Schedule"** on the Dairy card to view line items.
- [ ] Click **"Structure Financing & Loan Routing"**.

### Step 4: Smart Financial Engine & Scheme Router (`/finance`)
- [ ] Verify **Theoretical Project Cost (10% Rule)**: `₹10,00,000` (from ₹1,00,000 margin).
- [ ] Verify **Scheme Routing**: Automatically routed to **Term Loan Scheme** (8.0% p.a., 84 months, 6 months moratorium).
- [ ] Verify **Max Eligible Loan**: `₹9,00,000` (90% of ₹10,00,000).
- [ ] Verify **Required Contribution**: `₹1,00,000` (10.0%, fully covered by savings).
- [ ] Toggle **Quarterly Schedule** vs **Monthly EMI Equivalent** (`₹15,165/mo`).
- [ ] Inspect the **Amortization Table**: Notice the 6-month moratorium period where interest is capitalized.
- [ ] Read the critical warning: **Eligibility ≠ Cash-Flow Affordability**.
- [ ] Click **"Run Business Survival Simulator"**.

### Step 5: Business Survival Simulator (`/simulator`)
- [ ] Examine the **Baseline Case**: DSCR = **1.45x** (Healthy institutional buffer > 1.25).
- [ ] Move the **Sales Decline Slider** to `-20%`:
  - Watch Stressed CADS drop sharply.
  - Watch Stressed DSCR fall to **0.88x** (< 1.00 Deficit Warning).
  - The bar chart dynamically reflects the compressed cash flow.
- [ ] Review the **Algorithmic Resizing Guidance**:
  - The system warns that ₹9,00,000 is unaffordable under stress.
  - Recommends resizing the loan downward to **~₹6,50,000** (2 milch animals) to maintain a safe DSCR ≥ 1.15x.
- [ ] Click **"Consult AI Business Advisor"**.

### Step 6: AI Business Advisor (`/advisor`)
- [ ] Click preset question: *"Why did my Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) decrease after the sales decline?"*
- [ ] Review the plain-language mathematical explanation of fixed vs variable costs.
- [ ] Toggle **Language to తెలుగు (Telugu)** on the top bar: Ask a question in Telugu and see vernacular advice with identical financial figures.
- [ ] Notice the badge: **"Rule-Based Deterministic Fallback Active"** (no paid API key needed).
- [ ] Click **"Generate Bankable Business Blueprint"**.

### Step 7: Business Blueprint & Exportable Report (`/report`)
- [ ] Inspect the complete **25-section Detailed Project Report (DPR)**.
- [ ] Review the **90-Day Pre-Investment Execution Checklist**.
- [ ] Click **"Print / Save as PDF"**:
  - Notice the clean print stylesheet with formatted margins, tables, and no clipped elements.
